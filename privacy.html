Privacy Policy (English)

Effective October 15, 2026

What we collect: Nothing. DANJIRI GP - Moonstep Saga (iPhone and web) does not collect names, email addresses, location, device identifiers, or any other personal information. There are no ads, no third-party analytics, and no accounts.

Web hosting: When you open the web version, the hosting provider (such as GitHub Pages) automatically records standard connection data such as your IP address, browser type and access time. This is handled under the hosting provider's own privacy policy; the author of this game does not collect or use it.

Stored on your device only: Personal bests, time-trial ghosts, mini-game high scores and your language setting are saved only on your device (or in your browser for the web version). They are never sent anywhere, and are removed when you delete the app or clear the site data.

Payments: The game is completely free, with no in-app purchases.

Children: The game itself collects nothing. Apart from the hosting provider's connection records described above, no personal information is sent when children play.

Changes and contact: Any changes will be posted on this page. Contact: Don Erika on X (Twitter).
